### Welcome to BC TigerMath AI: Student Training Guide
TigerMath is your Socratic math partner. It will not just give you the answer; it will guide you to it!

1. HOW TO ASK QUESTIONS: 
   - Be specific about your math topic (e.g., "How do I factor this quadratic?").
   - Use the 'Insert Math Symbols' popover to add complex notation like integrals, limits, or exponents to your chat input.

2. GETTING UNSTUCK:
   - If you are lost, tell the AI: "I am completely stuck." It will provide a micro-explanation of the rule.
   - Use the 'Quick-Load' buttons for common algebra, calculus, or stats problems to see examples of how to format your questions.

3. LEARNING PATH:
   - The AI will provide links to study guides (external sources) when you discuss specific topics. Use these to reinforce your lesson plans!

4. EXPLORE MATH TOPICS & FORMULAS:
   - Expand the dropdown tool on your main dashboard to browse direct rules across Algebra, Trig, Calculus, and Statistics. Select a subtopic and click 'Explain' to immediately review the core formulas.

5. LANGUAGE & STYLE SETTINGS:
   - Open the sidebar configuration expander module to instantly translate the user interface (into Español, Français, or Deutsch) or input a custom persona/style for your AI tutor.
