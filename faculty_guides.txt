### Faculty Training Guide: Creating Lesson Plans with TigerMath
Use TigerMath to streamline your curriculum development:

1. LESSON PLAN DRAFTING: 
   Ask: "Draft a 30-minute lesson plan for [Topic, e.g., Quadratic Factoring] suitable for a college-level algebra class."

2. DIFFERENTIATED HINTS: 
   Ask the AI to generate hints at different difficulty levels: 
   - "Give me a 'Level 1' hint for a student struggling with basic arithmetic in this derivative problem."
   - "Give me a 'Level 3' hint that challenges a student to think about the conceptual rule."

3. ASSESSMENT CREATION: 
   Ask: "Generate 5 practice problems on [Topic] and provide an answer key with step-by-step explanations."

4. TEACHING STRATEGY:
   Ask: "What are some common misconceptions students have about [Topic], and how can I address them in class?"

5. EXPLORE MATH TOPICS & FORMULAS:
   - Use the interactive main dashboard module to quickly reference core department guidelines or review foundational formulas across Algebra, Trig, Calculus, and Statistics during lecture prep.

6. LANGUAGE & STYLE SETTINGS:
   - Use the sidebar parameters to translate your workspace into different languages or test custom persona rules to align the AI's instructional tone with your class curriculum.
